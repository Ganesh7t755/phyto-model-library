TRANSPARENT ARCHITECTURE

Your Device. Your Intelligence. Your Control.

We believe in honest boundaries. Here is exactly how Phyto Intelligence handles data between your phone and the cloud:

STRICTLY ON-DEVICE

Local Device Operations

  • Garden Archive: All saved specimens, photos, and notes are stored locally in your phone's Room database.
  • Offline AI Conversations: Every prompt and response with loaded GGUF models runs entirely on your CPU/RAM with zero network requests.
  • Downloaded AI Models: GGUF weights remain in your phone's local storage and are never uploaded anywhere.
ONLINE RECOGNITION

Cloud-Assisted Features

  • Camera Plant Identification: To achieve deep taxonomic accuracy, captured plant photos are sent securely to Google's Gemini cloud API for analysis.
  • Catalog OTA Updates: Periodically checks for botanical metadata updates to expand the plant dictionary over time.
  • Model Library Downloads: Downloading GGUF models streams files from official Hugging Face repositories directly to your device.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions & Answers

Phyto Intelligence is an open-source Android application designed for plant identification, botanical exploration, and on-device offline conversational intelligence. It allows you to recognize plant species using your camera, save discoveries to a local garden archive, and converse with local language models without an internet connection.

The application is designed with a hybrid architecture: Your saved Garden Archive and all Offline AI chat features run 100% on your device without internet access once a model is downloaded. Camera plant identification utilizes cloud-assisted AI (Google Gemini) for deep taxonomic precision, which requires an active data connection.

GGUF is a standardized, highly optimized binary file format designed by the llama.cpp project for running large language models directly on local consumer hardware (including smartphone CPUs). Phyto Intelligence embeds llama.cpp so you can run quantized botanical models right on your phone without remote servers.

No. Performance depends heavily on your phone's processor and available RAM. Light models (1B to 2.7B parameters, ~700 MB to 1.8 GB file size) run comfortably on most phones with 4 GB to 6 GB RAM. Reasoning models (3.8B to 8B parameters, 2 GB+ file size) require phones with 6 GB to 8 GB+ RAM for stable execution.

We host the official Phyto Model Library, featuring 10 curated and verified botanical models. When you download a model from the library, returning to the Phyto Intelligence app automatically detects the file in your downloads folder.

Distributing directly as an APK allows full freedom for native C++ binary execution (llama.cpp JNI bindings), rapid OTA model catalog synchronization, and direct user ownership without proprietary app-store restrictions.
